Why acting

An actor's training is, above all, a training in presence under attention.

For 30+ years, Sofia has worked with the same instrument leaders rely on every day: the body, the voice, and the ability to stay grounded while being watched.

This is not communication coaching layered over a script. It is the discipline actors use to be truthful in front of an audience — applied to the boardroom instead of the stage.

Different rooms. The same instrument.
